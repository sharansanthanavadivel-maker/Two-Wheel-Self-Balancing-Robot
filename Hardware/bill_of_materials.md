# Bill of materials

- Arduino Uno
- MPU6050 / GY-521 IMU module
- L298N dual H-bridge motor driver
- Two matched geared DC motors
- Two wheels and a two-wheel chassis with a suitable support/caster
- Battery matched to motor voltage/current requirements
- Power switch, wiring and mounting hardware

Select battery, motors and driver based on actual current draw. The L298N is an older bipolar driver with significant voltage drop and heat dissipation; verify it is appropriate for the motors used.
